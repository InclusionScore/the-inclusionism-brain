# Information Metabolism

## Definition
The process through which civilizations generate, exchange, interpret, and adapt through continuous informational flows.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Civilization behaves like an informational metabolism.

Participation generates data.
Data enables coordination.
Coordination generates value.
Value drives adaptation.
Adaptation reshapes civilization.
