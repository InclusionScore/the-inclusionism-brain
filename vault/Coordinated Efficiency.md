# # Coordinated Efficiency

## Definition

The capacity of a system to achieve desired outcomes through organized cooperation among participating agents.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Efficiency alone is not a sufficient measure of civilizational success.

---

## Core Principle

Healthy civilizations optimize for both efficiency and agency.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Systems often become more efficient by reducing participation.

Inclusionism questions whether gains in efficiency justify losses in agency, belonging, or legitimacy.

---

## Civilizational Tension
