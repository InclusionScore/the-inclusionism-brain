# Thermodynamic Economics

## Definition
An economic framework viewing civilization as a system of energy transformation, information exchange, and entropy management.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Economic systems are fundamentally coordination systems for organizing energy, information, labor, and participation.

---

## Core Principle
Value emerges through transformations that reduce uncertainty and increase adaptive capacity within connected systems.
