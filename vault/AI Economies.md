# # AI Economies

## Definition

Economic systems in which artificial intelligence, automation, data, and computational infrastructure become primary mechanisms of value creation.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

AI economies increase the importance of attribution, ownership, participation, and agency.

---

## Core Principle

As labor becomes less central to value creation, participation claims may increasingly arise from informational, relational, and civilizational contributions.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

AI economies force civilizations to reconsider:

- ownership
    
- labor
    
- participation
    
- agency
    
- value distribution
