# Inclusion

## Definition
The expansion of meaningful participation within coordinated systems.

---

## Core Principle
Inclusion increases coordination capacity.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Inclusion is the expansion of meaningful participation across systems historically constrained by stratification structures such as race, class, caste, geography, identity, and informational access.
