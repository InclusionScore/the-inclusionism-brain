# Relational Attribution

## Definition

The process of identifying and understanding the contributions of individual agents within systems where value emerges through interaction.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Relational value does not eliminate individual contribution.

It creates a need for increasingly sophisticated methods of understanding contribution.

---

## Core Principle

The fact that value emerges through relationships increases the importance of attribution.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Future civilizations may become increasingly capable of understanding how value emerges through complex networks of participation.
