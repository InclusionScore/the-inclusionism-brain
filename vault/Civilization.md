# # Civilization

## Definition

A large-scale coordination system through which agents organize interactions, value creation, participation, belonging, and governance.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Civilizations are systems for managing interactions among differentiated agents.

---

## Core Dynamic

Existence  
→ Interaction  
→ Witnessing  
→ Value  
→ Agency  
→ Participation  
→ Belonging  
→ Civilization

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Civilizations create:

- identity systems
    
- ownership systems
    
- governance systems
    
- value distribution systems
    
- belonging systems
    

These systems determine who participates, who belongs, and who possesses agency.

---

## Civilizational Success

Healthy civilizations increase:

- agency
    
- participation
    
- recognition
    
- legitimacy
    
- belonging
    

while preserving:

- differentiation
    
- pluralism
    
- innovation
    
- adaptability
