# Data Ontology

## Definition
A layered framework describing how raw interaction becomes meaning, intelligence, coordination, and value within civilization.

---

# Inclusionist Hierarchy

## Data
Observable residue generated through interaction.

---

## Information
Structured or organized data.

---

## Interpretation
The assignment of meaning or significance to information.

---

## Knowledge
Stable interpreted information retained across time.

---

## Intelligence
Adaptive coordination using knowledge.

---

## Value
Meaningful impact on systems, agents, or civilization.

---

## Wisdom
Long-term coordination capacity balancing adaptation, resilience, flourishing, and sustainability.
