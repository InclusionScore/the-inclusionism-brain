# Ownership Concentration

## Definition
The accumulation of ownership, productive assets, informational infrastructure, technological leverage, or coordination power within a relatively small portion of civilization.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Ownership concentration influences:
- participation capacity
- economic inclusion
- governance legitimacy
- social mobility
- informational access
- civilizational resilience

---

## Core Principle
Extreme ownership concentration can disconnect participation from value distribution.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
As automation and AI scale, ownership concentration may increasingly occur through:
- AI infrastructure
- computational resources
- network effects
- data monopolies
- algorithmic coordination systems
- informational platforms
