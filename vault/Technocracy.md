# Technocracy

## Definition
A system of governance in which decision-making authority is concentrated among technical experts, institutions, algorithms, or optimization systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Technocracy may improve coordination efficiency while simultaneously reducing democratic participation, pluralism, and distributed agency.

---

## Core Principle
Optimization does not automatically produce legitimacy.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Civilization requires balancing:
- expertise
- participation
- legitimacy
- pluralism
- adaptive coordination
