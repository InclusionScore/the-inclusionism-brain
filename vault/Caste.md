# Caste

## Definition
A rigid social stratification system in which participation, status, legitimacy, identity, and opportunity become inherited or structurally fixed across generations.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Caste systems reduce adaptive capacity by constraining participation mobility and civilizational inclusion.

---

## Core Principle
Civilizations become fragile when identity permanently determines participation capacity.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Modern caste-like systems may emerge through:
- inherited wealth
- algorithmic ranking
- informational exclusion
- social scoring systems
- technological monopolization
- network gatekeeping
