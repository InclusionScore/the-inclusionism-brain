# Information Manipulation

## Definition
The intentional shaping, distortion, filtering, amplification, suppression, or weaponization of informational flows in order to influence perception, coordination, behavior, or legitimacy.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Civilization increasingly depends on informational integrity for democratic participation, trust, and collective intelligence.

---

## Core Principle
Control over informational environments increasingly shapes:
- governance
- legitimacy
- economic power
- identity formation
- social coordination
