# Belonging Systems

## Definition
Structures through which individuals and groups establish, maintain, expand, or restrict membership, identity, participation, and recognition.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Belonging systems answer two foundational questions:

- Who belongs?
- Under what conditions?

---

## Examples

- religions
- nations
- families
- tribes
- companies
- political movements
- digital communities

---

## Core Principle

Every belonging system creates:
- inclusion
- exclusion
- identity
- participation rules
- legitimacy structures
