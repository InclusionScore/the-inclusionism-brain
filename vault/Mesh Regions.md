# Mesh Regions

## Definition
A mesh region is a networked zone of belonging, identity, exchange, and coordination that may overlap with but is not limited by nation-state borders.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Mesh regions replace rigid territorial thinking with dynamic networks of family, tribe, community, culture, data, and shared participation.
