# # Universal Basic Ownership

## Definition

A framework in which individuals possess baseline ownership participation within civilization-scale value-generating systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Universal Basic Ownership expands beyond income redistribution by distributing participation in ownership itself.

---

## Core Principle

Participation in civilization should generate pathways to ownership participation.

---

## Relationship to Universal Basic Income

Universal Basic Income distributes income.

Universal Basic Ownership distributes participation in ownership.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

As automation, AI, and informational economies become increasingly important, ownership distribution may become more significant than wage distribution.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Universal Basic Ownership seeks to preserve agency by ensuring that individuals retain meaningful claims within the systems generating value.
