# Cost of Belonging

## Definition

The social, economic, cultural, informational, or behavioral requirements necessary to gain and maintain membership within a belonging system.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Belonging systems compete partially through differences in participation cost.

---

## Examples

Low Cost:
- open communities
- universalist systems

High Cost:
- exclusive societies
- rigid caste systems
