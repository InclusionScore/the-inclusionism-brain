# The Problem Isn't Identity

## Inclusionist Thesis

The problem is not [[Identity]].

The problem is immature identity.

## Examples

White nationalism is immature identity.

Black supremacy is immature identity.

Religious fundamentalism is immature identity.

Ethnonationalism is immature identity.

Hyper-individualism is immature identity.

## Shared Pattern

Immature identities:

- require enemies
- require domination
- require [[Exclusion]]
- require hierarchy

## Mature Identities

Mature identities:

- know themselves
- possess continuity
- possess dignity
- seek relationship

## Inclusionist Horizon

The future is not post-identity.

The future is post-domination.

This connects identity maturation to [[Belonging]], [[Pluralism]], [[Recognition]], [[Agency]], and [[Legitimacy]].
