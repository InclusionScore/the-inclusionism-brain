# Participation Precedes Ownership

## Inclusionist Principle
Participation in systems precedes claims of ownership over systems.

---

## Explanation
Individuals and entities generate value through:
- existence
- interaction
- labor
- data generation
- coordination
- contribution

Ownership systems emerge afterward as mechanisms for organizing claims over value.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Civilizations become unstable when participation and ownership become disconnected.
