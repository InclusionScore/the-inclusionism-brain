# Privacy

## Definition
The capacity of individuals or systems to regulate informational exposure, interpretation, and access.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Privacy is not secrecy.

Privacy is sovereignty over informational existence.

## Informational Self-Determination

Informational self-determination is the capacity of agents to understand, contest, govern, and benefit from the data trails that extend from their lives.

It connects [[Privacy]] to [[Agency]], [[Self Sovereignty]], [[Personal Data]], [[Data]], [[Governance]], and [[AI Governance]].

Privacy protects agency when it prevents informational systems from defining, predicting, extracting from, or governing people without legitimate participation.
