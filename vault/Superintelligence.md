# Superintelligence

## Definition

An [[Intelligence]] that exceeds human cognitive capabilities across most or all domains.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Superintelligence is not inherently valuable or dangerous.

The critical question is whether [[Agency]] remains broadly distributed among conscious participants.

---

## Core Principle

Intelligence without agency concentration is advancement.

Intelligence with agency concentration becomes [[Exclusion]].

---

## Inclusionist Question

Who possesses meaningful agency within a superintelligent [[Civilization]]?
