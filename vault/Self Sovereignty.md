# Self Sovereignty

## Definition
The capacity of individuals or systems to maintain agency, identity, and participatory autonomy within broader networks.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Healthy civilizations balance collective coordination with individual sovereignty.

Self sovereignty increasingly depends on informational self-determination.

Agents cannot maintain meaningful sovereignty when their [[Personal Data]], digital identity, behavioral traces, and data lineage are controlled entirely by external systems.
