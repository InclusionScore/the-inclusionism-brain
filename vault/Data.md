# Data

## Definition
Information generated through the existence, behavior, interaction, and identification of individuals or systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Data is the connective tissue of network civilization and the micro-unit through which agency, identity, and value become visible.

## Data Lineage

Data has lineage.

It carries traces of the agents, contexts, interactions, environments, and transformations that produced it.

Data lineage matters because [[Relational Attribution]] becomes impossible when data is detached from the people, systems, and relationships that generated it.

Without lineage, data can become a vehicle for hidden [[Value Distribution]] and unrecognized extraction.
