# Adversarial Inquiry

## Definition
The intentional use of competing perspectives, criticism, and disagreement to strengthen the accuracy, resilience, and adaptive capacity of knowledge systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Healthy civilizations require disagreement.

Debate functions as a mechanism for:
- error correction
- innovation
- legitimacy
- collective intelligence

---

## Core Principle

Ideas that cannot survive critique cannot reliably guide civilization.
