# # Recognition

## Definition

The acknowledgment that an agent exists, participates, contributes, or belongs within a system.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Recognition transforms witnessing into social legitimacy.

---

## Core Principle

Participation becomes meaningful when it is recognized.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Many forms of exclusion emerge not from hostility but from failures of recognition.

Unrecognized participation often produces unrecognized value.
