# Witnessing

## Definition

The observation, acknowledgment, or recognition of existence through interaction.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Witnessing is the process through which value becomes knowable.

An agent may exist independently, but value emerges and becomes meaningful through interaction and observation.

---

## Core Principle

The value of existence becomes knowable through witnessing.

---

## Inclusionist Thought Experiment

In a universe containing only a single isolated object, existence may occur.

The moment another object exists, witnessing becomes possible.

With witnessing comes differentiation, interaction, information, and value emergence.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Civilizations expand belonging by increasing opportunities for meaningful witnessing.

Ignorance creates exclusion.

Witnessing creates the possibility of belonging.
