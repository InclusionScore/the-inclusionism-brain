# Exclusion

## Definition
The restriction of meaningful participation within systems of coordination and value creation.

---

## Inclusionist Principle
Exclusion reduces adaptive intelligence and system-wide value generation.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Exclusion reduces collective intelligence by limiting participation diversity, informational flow, and adaptive capacity.
