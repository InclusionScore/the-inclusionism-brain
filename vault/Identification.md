# Identification

## Definition

The process through which agents recognize, acknowledge, and incorporate one another into their understanding of a shared system.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Identification is the bridge between witnessing and belonging.

---

## Core Principle

Individuals are at their best when they identify with communities.

Communities are at their best when they identify all of their individuals.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Every act of identification changes both the observer and the observed.

Newly identified individuals introduce new perspectives, capabilities, and forms of participation into communities.
