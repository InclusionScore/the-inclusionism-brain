# Consciousness

## Definition
The capacity of a system to recognize, interpret, and integrate meaningful state changes.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Value requires recognition by conscious systems.

## Clarification

Inclusionism does not use consciousness primarily in a mystical or supernatural sense.

Instead, consciousness refers to the capacity of systems to:
- interpret information
- maintain subjective continuity
- model environments
- generate meaning
- participate adaptively within relational systems

This framework remains compatible with multiple philosophical interpretations of consciousness while focusing primarily on its functional and civilizational implications.
