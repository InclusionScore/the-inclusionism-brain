# Value Objects

## Definition

Transferable units of perceived significance that influence participation, belonging, coordination, or identity within systems.

---

## Examples

- trust
- forgiveness
- status
- identity
- covenant
- belonging
- recognition

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Value objects move through networks and influence participation behavior.
