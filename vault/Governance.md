# # Governance

## Definition

The processes through which systems coordinate participation, authority, decision-making, value distribution, and collective action.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Governance is the management of participation at scale.

---

## Core Principle

Governance exists to coordinate interactions among differentiated agents.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Every governance system must answer:

- Who participates?
    
- Who decides?
    
- Who benefits?
    
- Who belongs?
    
- How is legitimacy established?
    

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Future governance systems may increasingly rely upon:

- collective intelligence
    
- networked participation
    
- AI-assisted coordination
    
- dynamic representation
