# Distributed Ecosystems

## Definition
Interconnected systems of individuals, institutions, technologies, communities, and environments that coordinate without relying entirely on centralized authority.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Civilization is moving from state-centered hierarchy toward distributed ecosystems of participation and coordination.
