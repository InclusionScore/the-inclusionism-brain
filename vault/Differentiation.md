# Differentiation

## Definition

The process through which agents become distinct from one another through identity, behavior, capability, knowledge, perspective, or participation.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Differentiation is one of the primary sources of value emergence.

Without differentiation there can be interaction.

Without interaction there can be no novel value.

---

## Core Principle

Communities grow through the differentiation of their participants.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Belonging and differentiation are not opposites.

Belonging evolves through the continual incorporation of differentiated agents.

Differentiated agents can interpret the same interaction through different histories, vulnerabilities, relationships, and positions. These perspectives can reveal distinct consequences without becoming equally complete accounts. See [[Contextual Legitimacy and Relational Interpretation]].
