# Physics of Belonging

## Definition

The study of how belonging forms, propagates, scales, fragments, and transforms across human systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Civilizations are composed of nested belonging systems.

Participation follows belonging.

Coordination follows participation.

Civilization emerges from coordination.

---

## Core Dynamic

Belonging
→ Participation
→ Coordination
→ Value Emergence
→ Civilization
