# Fairness

## Definition

The perception that value, participation, opportunity, responsibility, and outcomes are being distributed in a legitimate manner.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Fairness emerges from legitimacy.

Legitimacy emerges from agency.

Agency emerges from recognition.

Recognition emerges from value.

Value emerges through interaction.

---

## Inclusionist Social Dynamic

Interaction  
→ Value  
→ Recognition  
→ Agency  
→ Legitimacy  
→ Fairness  
→ Belonging

---

## Core Principle

Fairness is not a fixed distribution.

Fairness is the perceived legitimacy of a distribution.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

People disagree about what is fair.

They are more likely to agree about whether they have been recognized.

Fairness therefore depends upon systems that accurately recognize participation and value creation.

Fairness also requires judgments that remain consistent in their commitment to agency without pretending that every interaction has the same relationships, risks, or consequences. See [[Contextual Legitimacy and Relational Interpretation]].
