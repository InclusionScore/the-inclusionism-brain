# Algorithmic Stratification

## Definition
The distribution of visibility, opportunity, legitimacy, participation, and influence through algorithmic systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Algorithms increasingly shape civilization-scale participation structures.

---

## Core Principle
Algorithmic systems are not neutral.

They encode:
- incentives
- priorities
- optimization goals
- historical bias
- governance structures
