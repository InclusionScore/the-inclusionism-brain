# Ownership

## Definition

The allocation of authority, responsibility, participation rights, and claims over value-generating systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Ownership is not merely possession.

Ownership is a relationship between agents and the value-generating systems in which they participate.

---

## Core Principle

Participation creates legitimate ownership claims.

Ownership follows contribution.

Contributors should hold meaningful claims in the systems, institutions, datasets, economies, and intelligence infrastructures they help create.

This does not mean every contribution creates identical ownership.

It means that ownership becomes less legitimate as it separates from [[Participation]], [[Relational Attribution]], and [[Value Distribution]].

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Value emerges relationally.

Ownership should therefore reflect both:

- individual differentiation
- relational contribution

---

## Ownership and Agency

Ownership is one mechanism through which civilizations distribute agency.

The concentration of ownership often produces the concentration of agency.

Ownership claims do not extend to another person's identity, experience, or interpretation. [[Contextual Legitimacy and Relational Interpretation]] preserves [[Self Sovereignty]] while examining how relational representations can still affect value, opportunity, and agency.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Healthy ownership systems balance:

- innovation incentives
- participation legitimacy
- agency distribution
- value recognition
