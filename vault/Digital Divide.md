# Digital Divide

## Definition
Unequal access to digital infrastructure, informational systems, computational tools, network participation, and technological leverage.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
The digital divide increasingly determines participation capacity within network civilization.

---

## Core Principle
Technological access increasingly shapes:
- economic participation
- educational opportunity
- political visibility
- informational agency
- collective intelligence participation
