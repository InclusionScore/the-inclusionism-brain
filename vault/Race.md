# Race

## Definition
A socially constructed system of human categorization historically associated with ancestry, physical traits, identity formation, power distribution, and civilizational hierarchy.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Race functions as a coordination and stratification system that shapes participation, recognition, belonging, and access within civilization.

---

## Core Principle
Race is socially real even when biologically imprecise.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Racial systems influence:
- institutional participation
- economic distribution
- informational visibility
- governance legitimacy
- identity formation
- social trust
- intergroup coordination
