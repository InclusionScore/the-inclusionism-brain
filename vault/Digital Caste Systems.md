# Digital Caste Systems

## Definition
Rigid or semi-rigid systems of informational, economic, social, or algorithmic stratification emerging through network civilization.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Future caste systems may emerge through:
- algorithmic ranking
- surveillance systems
- informational monopolies
- AI optimization
- social scoring
- inherited technological leverage

---

## Core Principle
Civilizations become fragile when participation capacity becomes structurally locked.
