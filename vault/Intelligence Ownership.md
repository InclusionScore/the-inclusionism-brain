# # Intelligence Ownership

## Definition

The ownership, governance, participation rights, and value claims associated with intelligence-producing systems.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

As intelligence increasingly becomes collective, computational, and networked, ownership questions become inseparable from participation legitimacy.

---

## Core Principle

The ownership of intelligence-producing systems influences the distribution of agency throughout civilization.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Artificial intelligence may become a form of civilization-scale infrastructure.

The governance of that infrastructure may become one of the defining questions of future civilizations.
