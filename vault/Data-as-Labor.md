# Data-as-Labor

## Definition
The recognition that human-generated data contributes economic value and should participate in systems of ownership and compensation.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Data production is a form of participation within networked economies.

Data-as-Labor is useful but incomplete.

Data is not only labor.

It may also be [[Personal Data]], identity extension, relational evidence, and the basis for [[Relational Attribution]].

The Inclusionist question is not only whether data should be compensated, but whether data contributors retain [[Agency]] and [[Ownership]] in the systems their data helps build.
