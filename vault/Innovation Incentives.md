# Innovation Incentives  
  
## Definition  
The structures, rewards, motivations, and participation systems that encourage experimentation, creativity, adaptation, invention, and civilizational advancement.  
  
---  
  
## Inclusionist Perspective  
Innovation requires both:  
- incentive structures  
- broad participation capacity  
  
Healthy civilizations maximize adaptive experimentation without collapsing inclusion.  
  
---  
  
## Core Principle  
Innovation systems become fragile when participation access becomes excessively restricted.  
  
---  
  
## Inclusionist Perspective  
Innovation may be incentivized through:  
- ownership opportunity  
- recognition  
- meaning  
- collaboration  
- curiosity  
- economic reward  
- civilizational contribution  
- technological participation  
  
---  
  
## Inclusionist Perspective  
Extreme ownership concentration may increase short-term efficiency while reducing long-term innovation diversity.
