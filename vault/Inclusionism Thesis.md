# Inclusionism Thesis

## Core Claim
Human civilization is evolving from industrial extraction systems toward networked coordination systems.

---

## Foundational Premise
Value emerges through recognized transformational interactions between conscious systems.

---

## Inclusionist Hypothesis
Inclusion increases adaptive intelligence, coordination capacity, and civilizational resilience.

---

## Economic Thesis
Post-industrial economies must distribute ownership, agency, and participation alongside automation.

---

## Technological Thesis
Technology should expand participatory agency rather than concentrate systemic power.

---

## Civilizational Thesis
The future of civilization depends on balancing connectivity, decentralization, intelligence, and belonging.

---

## Existential Thesis
Meaning emerges through participation in increasingly interconnected systems of shared transformation.
