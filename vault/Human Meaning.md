# # Human Meaning

## Definition

The experience of significance, purpose, contribution, belonging, and agency within reality.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Meaning emerges through participation in systems that individuals perceive as valuable.

---

## Core Principle

Agency is one of the primary foundations of meaning.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Participation without agency risks becoming performative.

Optimization without meaning risks becoming alienating.

Civilizations must balance efficiency with significance.
