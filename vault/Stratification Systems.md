# Stratification Systems

## Definition
Structures that distribute participation, legitimacy, agency, ownership, mobility, and recognition unevenly across civilizations.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Stratification systems influence:
- coordination capacity
- social trust
- collective intelligence
- adaptive resilience
- informational access
- participation legitimacy

---

## Major Systems
- [[Race]]
- [[Class]]
- [[Caste]]

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Healthy civilizations reduce rigid exclusionary stratification while preserving pluralism, identity, and adaptive diversity.

---
