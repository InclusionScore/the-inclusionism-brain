# Social Mobility

## Definition
The capacity of individuals or groups to change their position within systems of participation, ownership, recognition, and economic or social stratification.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Healthy civilizations maintain pathways for adaptive participation mobility rather than permanently fixing individuals within inherited structures.

---

## Core Principle
Civilizational resilience increases when participation pathways remain open.
