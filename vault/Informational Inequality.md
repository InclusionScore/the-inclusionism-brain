# Informational Inequality

## Definition
Unequal access to information, informational infrastructure, interpretation capacity, visibility, technological leverage, and network participation.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective
Informational inequality increasingly shapes civilizational power distribution in network society.

---

## Core Principle
Control over information systems increasingly determines:
- agency
- participation
- ownership
- economic leverage
- governance influence
