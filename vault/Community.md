# # Community

## Definition

A group of agents connected through shared participation, recognition, identity, purpose, geography, values, or relationships.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Communities are dynamic systems rather than static memberships.

---

## Core Principle

Communities evolve through the continual recognition of new participants.

---

## Inclusionist Perspective

Communities are healthiest when they preserve both:

- belonging
    
- differentiation
    

Uniformity weakens adaptability.

Differentiation generates new forms of value.
